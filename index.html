Water Pipes of Farawayland

Rules

  • Rotate the pipes so water reaches every consumer (indicated by circles).
  • For the consumer endpoints, the side with the capped line (╹) should connect to the other pipes.
  • Every pipe must be connected to the source (indicated by red cell).
  • If the water flow is connected, the cell will turn blue.
  • If the water flow is disconnected, the cell will remain white.
  • The network must not contain cycles (do not connect the pipes so they form a complete loop).
  • Neighboring pipes must connect correctly, and open ends must not leak.
